## Position and boundary

The site should help owners, architects, hospitality operators, communities, contractors, and buyers brief, design, procure, install, and maintain timber buildings. It does not provide structural/geotechnical/fire/electrical sign-off, cultural authority, permit rulings, universal member sizes, or unsupported durability/pricing claims.

## Evidence gates

- Loads, spans, member sizes, joints, anchors, foundations, wind/seismic, fire, occupancy, accessibility, electrical, plumbing, and public-use capacity require competent design, current standards, and approvals.
- Species, grade, moisture, treatment, termite/fungal resistance, legal origin, emissions, coating, and durability require traceable batch/product evidence.
- Traditional/cultural forms, motifs, sacred uses, and community claims require consultation and permission; visual imitation is not cultural verification.
- Prices, quantities, schedules, energy, service life, and warranties require dated like-for-like evidence.
- Settlement, movement, rot, pests, fire damage, failed connections, or electrical symptoms require isolation and qualified assessment.
